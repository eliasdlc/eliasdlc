## Products

- **[Kino](https://usekino.dev)**: a planner that learns your real energy curve and shapes your day around it. An agent can operate it through MCP. `TypeScript`
- **[ConoceRD](https://github.com/eliasdlc/conocerd-apk)**: Android app. Download the latest preview APK from the releases page.
- **[mikampus](https://github.com/eliasdlc/mikampus-better-solution)**: a local platform that replaces the day-to-day of the PUCMM student portal: course search, schedule builder and enrollment, with the portal scraped through Playwright. `HTML`
